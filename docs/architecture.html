FamilyOS

FamilyOS reads the notices a household already gets (school circulars, bills, receipts, forms), works out what each one asks of a person, and proposes dates, tasks and reminders that someone accepts, stopping one tap short of paying or booking.

  • Never holds a card, a UPI PIN or a bank login, and never pays
  • A person approves every date, task and errand
  • Projection, not twin: every fact points back to its notice

    What is built, and what is planned

    Built and exercised

    • Intake from share sheet, upload, forwarded mail, Gmail, Telegram and WhatsApp forwards. Gateway, quarantine, consent, dedup.
    • The reader with rules, local or cloud models, grounding, revisions and attribution. Scored against evalset/.
    • Brief, inbox, reminders, Sunday digest, Google Calendar writes, Ask with proposals.
    • Money ledger from receipts, the loop closer, pay links (phase one of paying).
    • Errands on Urban Company with sealed sessions, runbook, generalist fallback and live hand-over.
    • Three browsers: Steel, local Chromium, the phone's web view.
    • The phone driver reading the school app. Run on a real iPhone on 9 Oct 2026; the cold-start fix is unit-tested, not yet re-run on the phone.
    • Consent, subject and household erasure, export, append-only audit.

    Planned, not built

    • Push notifications and universal links. Need a paid team and a real host.
    • Phone actions over Telegram with an allow-list.
    • Errands on Instamart, Swiggy and the school portal; a second guardian taking over.
    • UPI AutoPay mandates, then scoped virtual cards. The card wall stays.
    • DigiLocker as a Requester. Researched 8 Oct 2026.
    • Production browsers: one per run, default-deny egress, an authenticated endpoint.
    • Reading the school app on a schedule; the session token off the socket's query string.
    • Tamil and Hindi; Android; rate limiting in front.

    Where the walls bite, honestly

    • No model reads a page holds for runbooks: a model answers at most one closed question over a short list that code extracted. The generalist, the fallback for a site with no runbook, does read the page through Anthropic's browser toolset; a gate refuses any click that would pay, order or confirm. It is skipped when the household has local models switched on.
    • Sealed covers originals, Gmail tokens and site sessions. Derived facts (claims, obligations) are plain rows protected by database controls.
    • Platform walls no one can engineer around: no API reads a WhatsApp group, school apps have no API, and iOS has no notification access. Forwarding, the phone driver and the Shortcuts trigger exist because of them.

    Glossary

    Artifact
    Something that arrived, exactly as it arrived: a mail, a PDF, a photo, a message. Stored once, sealed, never rewritten.
    Claim
    One fact read out of an artifact, with the words it came from and where they sit on the page.
    Obligation
    What the family might do about a claim: a task or a calendar date. It starts proposed; a member accepts or dismisses it.
    Errand
    A booking, a purchase or a look on a site with no API, written down in full and run only after a guardian approves it.
    Runbook
    A fixed script for one site: go here, read the words, press this. It never shows the page to a model and hands over to the person when stuck.
    Sealed session
    The cookies a member's own sign-in left for one listed site, sealed under the household key and opened only for the length of one run.
    Guardian
    An adult who can record consent for a child, approve errands, accept quarantined mail and change the household's rules.
    Consent
    A guardian's recorded permission to hold anything about a child. Without it nothing can name the child; withdrawing it erases what did.
    Projection, not twin
    Everything FamilyOS shows is computed from the sealed originals and their claims. There is no merged memory that could outlive an erasure or lose its source.
