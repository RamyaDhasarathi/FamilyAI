Ask

Just ask the home.

Ask about a bill, a dose or a date. Each answer carries a number that opens the exact notice it was read from.

When it is unsure, it asks you which one you meant. When it wants to act, it shows a card and waits.

Everything else it does

The quiet work around the edges.

Family map

Click through how it all connects: people, dates, documents, warranties, medicines and shops, with the notice behind every link.

Heads-ups

It tells you what nobody asked: a pile of dated things for one child, a prescription tied to no one, a warranty about to end. Each is sent once, and it respects your quiet hours.

Documents and warranties

Expiring licenses, policies and warranties become renewals and cards. An invoice that states a warranty is filled in for you to confirm.

Teach by showing

Show it a task once, on a phone app or a public page, and it keeps the steps. It only ever opens, taps and reads; it never pays or submits.

Your assistant, both ways

Connect Claude, Grok or any MCP assistant with your own key to read and propose, or send it an instruction from FamilyOS and read the answer. You confirm everything.

Invite the family

Private by design

It notices. You decide.

FamilyOS stops one tap short of doing. Nothing is sent, booked or paid without a person’s yes.

Optional: hold the key as a family. One tap makes a recovery phrase for you to keep; each trusted device then opens the household with a passkey. Or split the key into word shares, any two of three. We keep neither, so we can’t unwrap the key ourselves. It protects your original files, your mailbox connection and your signed-in sessions at rest and in backups, and the text FamilyOS reads from them can be sealed under the same key. Unlocked, FamilyOS works as usual; locked, it waits.

What we don’t claim: while your household is unlocked we read what you share so we can help, and the AI that reads it sees what it is sent.

Every fact has a source

Dates, amounts and doses quote the line they were read from. If it cannot ground a fact, it shows nothing.

Your key, your home

Originals are sealed with a key for your household. Destroy the key and the record is unreadable.

Shared on purpose

Place, only if you opt in

Adults can share where they are, each their own choice, with an alert on arriving home.
