The line we hold

A short list of things it will not do.

Most family tools get faster by doing more on their own. FamilyOS gets trusted by stopping where a person should decide.

The important thing is not that AI can act. It is that your family always knows why.

Spend money on its ownPayments are off until a guardian switches them on, capped per purchase and per month, and each purchase needs a guardian’s approval.
Hold a card number or a passwordThe payment provider holds the card. FamilyOS keeps only an id.
Read a group chat it was not asked intoIn a Telegram group it reads only a message that names it or replies to it, and says nothing about the rest.
Keep a trail of where you goSharing is yours to switch on and off. Off deletes the point at once.
Show a fact it cannot point back toA date it cannot ground in the original stays visible for review and becomes nothing.
Privacy by construction

A record you can check and a key you can destroy.

  1. ObserveOnly the sources you chose

  3. ProposeA clear next step, never a surprise
  4. You approveNothing important skips this
  5. Follow throughWith a reminder and a record

Derived, never merged

What FamilyOS knows about your household is computed from the notices you shared, each sealed with your household’s key. Destroy the key and what it knew becomes unreadable, because it was only ever derived.

Read by a model on your machine, on a server you run, or by a cloud provider. You choose, and the setting tells you where it runs.
